Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use the app, we store the expense records you create, including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — to display your expense history, generate summaries, and personalise your experience. We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to any third party.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly uses Google Fonts to load typefaces. This causes your browser to make a request to Google's servers. No expense or account data is shared with any third-party service.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
